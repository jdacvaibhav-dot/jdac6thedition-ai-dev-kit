## Usage notes
- **Logo Green `#ADFF26`** is a bright lime. It has high contrast on dark surfaces but **fails as text on light/near-white backgrounds** — use it as a fill, accent strip, or text-on-dark only. For green-colored text on light slides, substitute Logo Black `#141712` or Charcoal `#222F2E`.
- **Logo Black `#141712`** is the deepest brand tone — use for primary text on light surfaces and for component dark fills.
- **Charcoal `#222F2E`** is the dark-mode background.
- Accent colors are a sanctioned categorical palette (Navy / Purple / Fire / Blue / Brown). Use them only to differentiate categories; never as decoration. **Fire `#FF4B00`** doubles as the negative/error signal; **Blue `#0076FF`** as informational.
